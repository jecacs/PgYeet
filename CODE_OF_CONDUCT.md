# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PgYeet's contributors and maintainers are committed to making participation in the project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its community;
- keeping technical disagreement factual and proportionate.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- threats, intimidation, or encouraging harmful behavior;
- sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or project operations;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, code
reviews, release channels, and community events. It also applies when an individual officially
represents the project in a public space.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the
contact method listed on the [PgYeet NuGet page](https://www.nuget.org/packages/PgYeet) under
**Contact owners**.

Do not include sensitive conduct details in a public issue. If no private contact method is available,
open a neutral issue asking the maintainer to establish private contact, without identifying people or
describing the incident publicly.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. The maintainer will respect the
privacy and safety of the reporter and other affected people.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this
Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, impact, and whether the participant
takes responsibility and changes their behavior. Possible responses include:

1. A private correction or warning.
2. A temporary restriction from interaction or contribution.
3. Removal of content or closure of a discussion.
4. A permanent ban from project spaces.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is itself a violation.
